1. The Core Problem in Traditional Betting

Unfair Risk to Reward Ratio: In conventional bookmakers and online casinos, users face completely asymmetric math against the house. For instance, when betting on a low risk 1.3x multiplier, the winner only captures a tiny +0.3x profit, while the losing player suffers an absolute 100% loss (0x payout).

This predatory structure forces players to risk their entire capital for meager returns, guaranteeing that the mathematical edge drains user balances over time.

2. The PvP Arena Solution: Pure Peer to Peer

To eliminate unfair house edges and deliver maximum profitability with downside risk mitigation, PvP Arena was architected exclusively as a 1v1 Player vs Player (P2P) protocol:

Superior Math & Asymmetric Rewards:

• Winner Multiplier (+0.9x Net Profit / 1.8x Return): The victor claims 90% of the entire 2 player prize pool, generating a massive +0.9x net profit on their stake (e.g., $100 entry wins $180 total).

• Loser Loss Protection (+0.03x Cashback): The defeated combatant never walks away with zero. The smart contract automatically refunds a 3% cashback rebate directly to the player wallet.

• Transparent Protocol Fee (7%): A minimal 7% fee covers decentralized infrastructure, reward pools, and automated token buyback & burn mechanisms.

3. Innovative Leaderboard Reward System (Dual Pool Incentive)

Unlike traditional casinos that punish losing streaks, PvP Arena incentivizes active participants through two separate weekly reward mechanisms funded by protocol fees:

🏆 Champion Leaderboard (Most Wins):

The top gladiators with the highest number of verified wins share a dedicated weekly prize pool paid out in $PVP tokens.

🛡️ Iron Heart & Bad Luck Protection (Top 3 Most Losses):

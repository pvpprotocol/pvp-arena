Protocol Rules & Architecture Solana Anchor Program
1. Fair 1v1 Matchmaking & PDA Escrow
Players join a matchmaking queue by locking their chosen ANSEM tier into a non-custodial Program Derived Address (PDA) escrow. The protocol pairs players in the same tier on a first-come, first-served basis.
2. Reward Distribution Breakdown
Every match combines two equal stakes (200% pool). Settlements are executed in the exact same block:
• 90% to Winner: 1.8x payout instantly transferred to the winning wallet.
• 3% Loser Cashback: Direct loss-protection cashback refunded to the loser's wallet.
• 7% Protocol Treasury: Distributed to buyback & burns and development.
• 100% Cancel Refund: If a player cancels queue before being matched, 100% of their stake is instantly unlocked.
3. Verified Token Contract
The PvP Arena protocol settles all wagers natively using the official $PVP token on Solana.

PVP Protocol Whitepaper

Official Architecture & Mathematical Specifications on Robinhood Chain

1. 1v1 Instant Duel Escrow Architecture

Traditional Betting vs PVP Architecture risk to reward: In conventional betting platforms, participants endure a highly unfavorable risk to reward ratio. For instance, with a 1.3x multiplier, the winner risks 100% of their principal to make a mere 30% profit, while the loser loses 100% of their capital. In the PVP Arena protocol, this imbalance is solved: the winner gains a direct 90% profit, while the runner up receives a 3% rebate cashback so they always retain capital and a second chance to re-enter and compete.

Two participants lock equal collateral into the canonical escrow smart contract (0x1e7e733d3fc72051f719537473f68f2204832dae). Upon verified outcome resolution, funds are automatically settled onchain:

  • 90% net payout allocated directly to the verified winner
  • 3% rebate cashback returned to the runner up wallet
  • 7% protocol treasury fee for buybacks and liquidity support

2. 2048 Skill Vaults & 24 Hour UTC Tournament Cycle

Skill tournaments run in continuous 24 hour cycles ending at 00:00 UTC every day.

  • Single High Score Record (No Accumulation): Scores from multiple games are never summed together. Only a player highest single run record qualifies.
  • Unlimited Ticket Purchases: Players may buy as many attempts as desired to improve their top record.
  • Special $PVP Token Perk: Purchasing tickets using the project native $PVP token grants 2x tickets (2 game attempts) per 1 ticket fee.
  • Reward Distribution: 80% of the entire tier vault is transferred to the top verified record holder, with 20% allocated as protocol fee.

3. Grand Jackpot multi target System & 1 Hour Rounds

Jackpot rounds operate on strict 1 hour countdown cycles. When the countdown timer reaches zero, round submissions automatically lock.

  • multi target Prediction: Supports measurable market prices, numeric indices, and quantifiable onchain targets.
  • Max 3 Tickets Per Wallet: Strict per wallet cap to prevent whale dominance and guarantee fair distribution.
  • Prize Settlement: 80% of the vault goes to the wallet with the closest numeric prediction, with 20% protocol fee.

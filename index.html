1. The Asymmetric Risk Problem

Traditional wagering platforms utilize an unbalanced risk to reward structure against users. When participating in a 1.3x multiplier wager, the winner captures only +0.3x (+30% gain), while the losing participant suffers an absolute 100% loss (0x payout). This house edge continuously degrades user capital over repeated sessions.

2. Pure Peer to Peer Protocol (1v1 Duels)

PvP Arena establishes direct 1v1 onchain settlement mechanisms without house custody:

• Winner Multiplier (+0.90x Net / 1.80x Return): 90% of the total 2 player pool is distributed to the winner.
• Loss Protection (+0.03x Rebate): Defeated participants receive an automated 3% USDC cashback rebate.
• Protocol Fee (7%): Supports smart contract upkeep, reward distributions, and token buybacks.

3. Grand Jackpot Pools & Multi Tier Dedicated Leaderboards

Community jackpot vaults across 5 completely independent stake levels ($1, $5, $10, $100, $1,000 USDC) each with their own isolated prize pool and dedicated leaderboard:

• Isolated Vault Pools & Rewards: Participants in a given tier (e.g. $1 Vault) compete solely against entries within that same tier and capture 80% of that specific vault's prize pool.
• Generalized Target Mechanism: Jackpot vaults support quantifiable target numbers and event metrics, extending beyond simple price queries.
• Strict Wallet Protection: Maximum limit of exactly 3 tickets per wallet address per tier vault to prevent whale dominance.
• Winning Resolution (80% / 20% Split): The participant submitting the closest recorded prediction to the verified final result captures 80% of the selected tier vault.
• Protocol Allocation (20%): 20% is routed to the protocol treasury for ongoing development, community liquidity, and deflationary token buybacks.

4. 2048 Skill Tournaments & Dedicated Tier Leaderboards

Competitive skill based tournament vaults across 5 independent tiers ($1, $5, $10, $100, $1,000):

• Dedicated Tier Leaderboards: Buying a ticket for the $1 Skill Vault enters your record exclusively into the $1 Leaderboard to win 80% of the $1 Vault prize. Higher tiers ($5, $10, $100, $1,000) have their own separate leaderboards.
• Strict Record Rule (No Score Accumulation): Game scores never accumulate over time. Only each player's highest verified single game record score is submitted to the vault leaderboard.
• Unlimited Ticket Purchases: Participants may purchase unlimited tickets and attempts to beat their personal record and surpass competitors.
• Project Token ($PVP) Perk: Purchasing tickets using the native $PVP token grants 2 Game Tickets (2 Attempts) for the price of 1.
• Tournament Winner (80%): The participant holding the highest verified single run record in that specific tier captures 80% of that tier's total vault pool.

5. Trustless Escrow Lifecycle & Anti Rug Guarantee

Pre Match Cancellation: When Player 1 opens a duel, their USDC is deposited in escrow. If no opponent joins, Player 1 can cancel at any moment to reclaim 100% of their deposited capital with zero penalties.

Duel Execution: When Player 2 joins, the match locks until timeframe expiry, whereupon the smart contract executes settlement directly to player wallets.

6. Dual Incentive Design

Protocol fees fund two distinct weekly reward paths:

• Champion Pool: Distributed to top verified win counts.

• Resilience Grants: Awarded to the Top 3 participants with highest weekly losses to sustain participant liquidity.

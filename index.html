1. Protocol Architecture

PvP Arena is a non-custodial, peer-to-peer 1v1 wagering protocol. Combatants lock equal entry stakes into a smart escrow contract. Match resolution uses verifiable randomness to ensure transparent and tamper-proof outcomes.

2. Fair Tokenomics & Reward Split

Every match pool is distributed transparently:

• 90% to Winner: The victorious gladiator receives 1.8x of their original stake.

• 3% Loss Protection Cashback: The defeated player receives an instant 3% cashback rebate.

• 7% Protocol Fee: Dedicated to platform maintenance and automated token buyback & burn.
